## 2. Macro data points

### Size & growth
- 2025 full year: mobile game revenue **$82B, +1% YoY** — flat (Sensor Tower State of Gaming 2026: https://sensortower.com/report/state-of-gaming-2026; https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/mobile-games-82-billion-revenue-2025-sensor-tower-report). Gaming overall (PC+console+mobile tracked) $94B, downloads 52B (https://finance.yahoo.com/news/sensor-tower-state-gaming-gaming-151700611.html).
- **H1 2026: IAP revenue ~$40B, DOWN 2% YoY** — first meaningful reversal (https://www.mobilemarketingreads.com/mobile-gaming-in-app-purchase-revenue-declined-2-in-h1-2026-as-ad-spend-reached-new-highs/).
- **Downloads collapsing: H1 2026 ~22B, -12% YoY** (-3B units). Rolling 4-quarter downloads: ~56B (Q3 2023) → ~46B (Q2 2026), **-17%**. ~94% of the decline is Android, ~80% is Tier 2/3 markets (India, Brazil, Indonesia, Mexico, Turkey). Revenue-per-download +11% YoY — monetization is masking shrinking reach. (Naavik: https://naavik.substack.com/p/h1-2026-mobile-gamings-stability)
- Non-gaming apps **overtook games** in global IAP revenue in 2025: $85.6B vs $81.8B (DoF: https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/2025/12/31/12-gaming-predictions-for-2026).
- **Games 5+ years old generate ~57% of top-200 revenue.** Only ~1 in 4 young top-200 entrants stays there long-term (Naavik H1 2026).
- Web shops / DTC: ~$17B in 2025, ~15% of market, still shifting revenue off-store (Naavik; DoF).

### Ads vs IAP mix
- Global ad spend on games H1 2026: **$7B, +7% YoY** while IAP fell — hybrid monetization is the direction of travel (mobilemarketingreads, above).
- Mobile game in-app AD revenue exceeded **$12B in 2025** (https://gameworldobserver.com/2026/07/06/in-2025-mobile-game-advertising-revenue-exceeded-12-billion-analytics).
- Share of games with ad monetization: 45.1% (Jul 2021) → **55.6% (May 2026)** (Tenjin: https://tenjin.com/blog/ad-mon-gaming-2026/).
- Regional split is stark: ads = **70.3% of game revenue in India** vs **22.1% in the US** (Jan–May 2026). Emerging markets 55–70% ads; US/JP/KR up to ~90% IAP.
- 83% of top games combine **5–7 monetization mechanisms** (Sensor Tower 2026). Hybrid-casual splits ~59% IAP / 41% ads.
- Ad revenue by segment (Feb–Apr 2026): casual ~40%, hyper-casual ~40%, hybrid 16%, mid-core 4%.

### UA / CPI
- Gaming CPI up ~30% YoY into 2026; average iOS CPI ~$4.22, Android ~$2.97 (https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-03-17-user-acquisition-cpi-benchmarks-2026/). Competitive mid-core titles: CPI can exceed $25 (DoF).
- Most expensive genres by CPI: slots ($4.47), idle RPG ($3.19). Match-3 economics now require D7 > 35% and CPI < $3 to profit against Royal Match.
- DoF 2026 predictions: 2–4% market growth, downloads -8%/yr, Chinese studios out-executing Turkish studios, VC retreat (M&A multiples down from ~10x to 1.5–3x revenue).

## 3. What's RISING (2025–2026)

### a) The puzzle renaissance — biggest genre gainer
Puzzle = **+$915M YoY in H1 2026**, the single largest genre gain while the market shrank (Naavik H1 2026):
- **Merge-2 + narrative: +$640M** — Gossip Harbor +$320M, Tasty Travels +$132M (both Century Games).
- **Sort games: +$303M** — Pixel Flow +$125M, Magic Sort (Grand Games) ~$50M/yr.

Naavik niche-subgenre deep dive (https://naavik.co/digest/how-niche-subgenres-are-reshaping-the-mobile-puzzle-market/), 2025 numbers:
- **Block jam**: ~$183M revenue, +2,900% YoY, but ~75% captured by one title (Color Block Jam, Rollic) — winner-take-all with UA muscle.
- **Sort**: ~$280M, +176% YoY, ~1B downloads, and revenue spread across a dozen+ titles each making $5–15M/yr — a "gentle slope," NOT winner-take-all. Naavik: the barrier here is "fundamentally creative rather than financial" → best subgenre shape for small teams.
- **Screw puzzles**: ~$206M, 2x YoY; Screwdom (iKame) ~45% share; best-in-class retention D30 ~17% vs block 4%, sort 8% → retention is the moat.
- Total puzzle market 2025: ~9.7B downloads, ~$10B IAP, +14% YoY.

### b) A genuinely NEW subgenre born within 12 months: "Arrows"
- **Arrows – Puzzle Escape**, small German studio Lessmore UG (Miniclip-owned), launched **July 2025**: tap arrows in order to clear a grid. 18M downloads by Mar 2026, ~170K installs/day, 4.7 stars — grown **entirely on organic search/ASO/word-of-mouth**, no paid UA. (https://www.capermint.com/blog/develop-game-like-arrows-puzzle-escape/)
- Chinese cloner (Learnings/Oakever, "Amaze Go") followed within months; BOTH are in the H1 2026 global top-10 downloads. Lesson: a novel one-rule puzzle mechanic can still create a chart-topping category from zero, and the clone window is ~2–6 months. (https://ixbt.games/en/news/2026/07/29/smena-lidera-i-novye-trendov-predstavlen-top-10-samyx-skacivaemyx-mobilnyx-igr-pervoi-poloviny-2026-goda.html)

### c) Hybrid-casual — the only structurally growing monetization segment
- Hybrid-casual IAP revenue **+20% to $4.2B in 2025** — the only segment to grow IAP; hybrid-casual overall revenue +37% (Sensor Tower via Udonis/Photon: https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-games/mobile-game-market-trends).
- **Habby template** (Capybara Go!: $100M gross in <3 months from Nov 2024 launch, $139M+ lifetime IAP; built by the same small internal team as Archero/Survivor!.io; Habby lifetime >$2B): simple core + roguelite meta + aggressive early IAP. (https://www.pocketgamer.biz/habbys-capybara-go-surpasses-100m-in-gross-player-spending/; https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/2025/7/31/habbys-hybridcasual-empire-the-template-that-built-a-powerhouse)
- **Supercent** (Korea): Pizza Ready! 221M+ downloads, ~70% ad / 30% IAP, $7–12M/mo across its top two titles; first Korean publisher into global top-10 by downloads. (https://www.gamigion.com/pizza-ready-by-supercent-comprehensive-breakdown/)

### d) Meme-culture / "brainrot" velocity games
- TikTok "Italian Brainrot" (early 2025) revived hyper-casual: Merge Fellas ~2.1M installs in 3 months off brainrot-themed updates; Sprunki (fan Incredibox mod) spawned an entire remix ecosystem. Culture-reactive reskinning = cheap organic installs. (https://foxdata.com/en/blogs/how-tiktoks-italian-brainrot-trend-revived-hypercasual-mobile-games-in-2025/)

### e) Underserved demographics: seniors
- **Vita Mahjong "for Seniors"** (launched Nov 2024): **360M lifetime downloads, ~21M/month**, #1 board game — big tiles, no timers, no fail pressure. An entire "Vita" line targets 55+ players, a fast-growing and almost uncontested audience. (https://www.appbrain.com/app/vita-mahjong/com.vitastudio.mahjong)

### f) UGC platforms absorbing youth + spend
- Roblox 2025: bookings **$6.8B +55%**, 144M DAU +69%, dev payouts $1.5B (+70%); **Roblox alone = two-thirds of the industry's net non-China consumer spending growth in 2025** (Naavik: https://naavik.co/deep-dives/the-state-of-ugc-games-2026/).
- **Grow a Garden**: built by a 16-year-old in ~3 days → 21.3M CCU (June 2025), most-played game ever by CCU; **Steal a Brainrot** then hit 25.4M CCU (Oct 2025). Half of 2025 Roblox spend came from games released that same year — the platform still rewards brand-new small-team content, unlike app stores. Top 1,000 Roblox creators average $1.3M/yr.

### g) Other risers
- Shooters +$218M H1 2026, but purely Tencent-in-China (Delta Force, Valorant Mobile) — geography-locked.
- IP gacha can still break in: **hololive Dreams** (CyberAgent QualiArts × COVER, July 23 2026; 1.5M pre-registrations) entered global top-10 revenue in its launch month — but requires a massive IP.
- Event-driven sports spikes (July 2026 World Cup pushed Soccer Superstar, Football League 2026 into top downloads).

## 5. Small teams vs big-UA-budget companies

### Where small teams have recently broken through
- **Block Blast!** (Hungry Studio, small Chinese team): ads-ONLY monetization, ~$584K/day (~$17.5M/mo), 870M lifetime downloads, 70M DAU / 300M MAU; #1 downloaded game of 2024 AND 2025. Ads-only + interstitial-light design = no UA war, pure retention play. (https://www.blog.udonis.co/statistics/block-blast; https://www.gamigion.com/block-blast-by-hungry-studio-is-doing-1m-a-day/)
- **Arrows – Puzzle Escape** (Lessmore, small German studio): top-10 global downloads with ZERO paid UA — organic ASO + novelty mechanic + handcrafted levels.
- **Vita Studio** (Oakever-affiliated): 360M downloads by targeting an ignored demographic (seniors) rather than a new mechanic.
- **Grow a Garden / Steal a Brainrot** (Roblox): teenage/small creators; biggest CCU numbers in gaming history; Roblox's discovery + social graph replaced UA spend entirely.
- **Sprunki** (solo fan-made mod, late 2024) → global meme ecosystem.
- **Kawaii Slime Drop** (solo dev, no art/programming background, 90+ AI-made assets) — modest but real (via Naavik AI-slop digest).
- Habby/Supercent occupy the middle: small dev teams, but publisher-scale UA — a "small team + template + publisher money" model.

### Where only big UA budgets win
- Match-3 IAP (Royal Match ~$100M/mo moat), casual casino (Monopoly GO! + Scopely/Savvy money), 4X survival (Century/FirstFun UA machines), AAA gacha (HoYo/CyberAgent production values), anything with CPI >$3 and payback >90 days. DoF: "only the largest players with sophisticated LiveOps and massive UA budgets can compete profitably" in these lanes; UA financing itself is commoditizing into big-capital hands.

### The pattern in every small-team win
1. **Novel-but-instant mechanic** (Arrows, Block Blast) or **novel audience** (Vita) or **novel culture-hook** (brainrot, Sprunki) — never a better version of an incumbent's game.
2. **Free distribution**: organic ASO, TikTok virality, or platform network effects (Roblox) — never paid UA.
3. **Ads-first or hybrid monetization** — no dependence on whale IAP depth at launch.
4. **Retention as the moat** (Block Blast 70M DAU; screw-puzzle D30 17%) — the only defensible metric once cloners arrive within 2–6 months.

## 6. Synthesis: opportunity gaps for a NEW game (mid-2026)

1. **Casual puzzle with one novel, instantly-legible rule** — the Arrows path. The market just proved (twice: Arrows, plus Sort/Screw waves) that new puzzle primitives can hit top-10 downloads on organic alone. Clone window ~2–6 months → plan the retention/content moat before launch.
2. **Sort-shaped subgenres** — distribution across many mid-size winners ($5–15M/yr each), explicitly the most small-team-accessible revenue pool per Naavik.
3. **Hybrid-casual meta on a casual core** (Habby/Supercent template): ~59/41 IAP/ads, dual currency, $1–3 starter packs, race events — the only IAP segment still growing.
4. **Underserved audiences**: seniors (Vita proved 55+ scale), and "calm/no-pressure" positioning generally — counter-programming against aggressive monetization fatigue.
5. **Culture-velocity games**: meme-reactive content pipelines (brainrot lesson) — speed as the differentiator, ship trend updates in days.
6. **Roblox as a parallel/first platform**: half of 2025 spend went to same-year releases; effectively the only storefront where new small-team content gets algorithmic reach and social distribution by default.
7. **Retention > everything**: with downloads -12% and 57% of revenue in 5+ year-old games, the market pays for games people keep, not games people try. D30 benchmarks: screw 17%, sort 8%, block 4%.
8. **Avoid entirely**: match-3, 4X survival, MMORPG/idle RPG, slots/casino, pure paid-UA hyper-casual — incumbent moats + CPI wars + declining pools.
