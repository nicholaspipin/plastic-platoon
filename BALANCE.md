# BALANCE.md — Directive 2 Tuning

## First 15 Minutes Beat Sheet

| Time | Beat | Tuning hook |
|---:|---|---|
| <30s | First upgrade purchase | `faster.base = 18`, starter stamps arrive quickly |
| ~90s | First platoon banner | stamp cadence + bigger mold make 20 greens fast |
| ~2m | First robot | boss cadence remains every 5 waves, early wave length reduced |
| ~4m | First LEGO gate breach | first checkpoint at 22% territory push |
| ~6m | First tier tease/purchase | bazooka silhouette unlock slot appears in bottom tray |
| 12-15m | First zone complete | Bedroom Carpet zone target tuned for active play, not overnight idling |

## Economy Principles

- Upgrade curves stay exponential, mostly `x1.55-x1.85`, so one purchase is usually close in the first session.
- Four verticals are visible early: mold speed, mold size, rifle damage, scout speed.
- Battalion reserve scales damage after the render cap, but visible banners/multipliers carry the fantasy instead of spawning infinite sprites.
- Hopper cap is 4.5 hours of current idle rate; full hopper becomes a gentle jam state.

## Directive 2 Tunables

- Mid-game rendered army target: 60-150 total figures.
- Formation platoon size: 20 soldiers.
- Default command line: 66% of screen width.
- Checkpoints: pencil bridge, LEGO gate, cereal-box fortress.
- First prestige preview surfaces once wave and push-speed walls are reached; first real prestige remains session 2-3.
